PRIVACY

Last updated: March 2026

What we collect

Sonarc does not require you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email or personal details. When you use our AI tools, your input (the text or settings you enter) is sent to our API to generate a response. We do not store these inputs.

Cookies & Analytics

We may use basic analytics (such as page views and country) to understand how people use Sonarc. This data is anonymous and not linked to any individual. We use Google AdSense to display ads, which may use cookies to show relevant advertisements.

Third parties

We use Groq's API to power our AI tools. Your prompts are processed by their servers according to their privacy policy. We do not sell your data to anyone.

Your rights

Since we don't store personal data, there is nothing to request or delete. If you have concerns, contact us at the email below.
